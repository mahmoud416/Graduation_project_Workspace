# Bubblein Landing Copy Baseline

## Hero Section
- Mention "Bubblein" by name within the first 60 characters.
- Reference automation or AI-driven QA in the first sentence.
- Promise a measurable outcome (ex: "cut QA cycle time by 40%").

## Proof Stack
- Include at least two quantified stats (customer logos, before/after metrics, or SLA guarantees).
- Highlight a regulated industry client (finance, health, gov) to reinforce trust.

## Call To Action
- Primary CTA text must be one of: "Start Free QA Audit", "Book QA Pilot", "Talk To QA Engineer".
- CTA button needs contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 against its container.

## Footer Compliance
- Provide contact email at the bottom.
- Link to the latest SOC-2 PDF stored in the trust center.
